# 2.0 new lines: design review

Sign-off table for the **66 new lines** and **3 new legendaries** (section 6 of `2.0-plan.md`). Once these are signed off, the art is generated in the 64 px remaster style and the lines join the traits review table.

- **Elements:** new lines go mostly to the elements with the fewest lines (Metal, Toxic, Shadow, Spirit). Every pairing of two elements that no line had yet now has one (38 new pairings).
- **Stats:** totals match today's lines (about 230 to 250). Lines of the thinner elements (Metal, Toxic, Shadow, Spirit, Dragon) get **244**, most others 240, and Fire (already the biggest element) 236.
- **Signature move:** what it does follows from its name, the same way as today's lines.
- **Body:** the body type the art is built on; the rest of the look is rolled and remastered like the current 120.

After this: 120 + 66 = **186 lines**, and 6 legendaries.

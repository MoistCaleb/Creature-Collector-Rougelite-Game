# Hexbound 2.0 plan

2.0 is one large update. Every section below is built on the working branch behind a single draft
PR, and nothing reaches main (or the live game) until all of 2.0 is ready. Fixes to the live game
still ship on their own from a short-lived branch and are merged into the 2.0 work.

Why 2.0: one run and you've "seen it all". Runs need their own character: builds that snowball
from choices made during the run.

Sections:

1. **Boons** (planned below)
2. **Traits** (planned below)
3. _next section: to be discussed_

## 1. Boons

Rule-bending perks for one run that stack into a build. 3–5 per run.

### Offers

- **When:** after each floor's guardian (3 per run, before the next floor); a **Boon hex** on about
  1 floor in 2 (at most one per floor, placed by a hash of the floor's layout like Revival, so the
  rest of the map is unchanged); in the Gilded Hunt, an offer every 2 hunt floors.
- **Choice of 3:** slot 1 matches an element on your team (weighted toward the weaker elements);
  slot 2 is a general boon (about 10% mythic); slot 3 is a curse about 25% of the time, else another
  general boon. No duplicates, nothing you already hold.
- **Seeded** like shop stock (`placeRng`): online races and daily/weekly runs offer everyone the
  same boons at the same points.
- **Online:** boons apply in stage battles and battle royales, including the final duel and the
  bracket (each player brings the boons they picked on their climb).
- **Guardians** get +1 level per boon held so stacked runs stay honest.

### Element boons (36)

Bot win rates by starter element (BALANCE_REPORT, 2026-11-19): Earth 20%, Dragon 18%, Light 18%,
Metal 17%, Water/Storm/Nature/Shadow 16%, Spirit/Toxic 15%, Fire/Frost 14%. The weaker elements get
more boons, and slightly stronger ones.

**Fire (4)**
- Kindling: Fire moves +20% burn chance; burned foes take +20% from Fire.
- Heat Wave: after a win, Fire Hexlings heal 15% (30% in sun).
- Ember Heart: Fire Hexlings can't be frozen and take 25% less from Water.
- Wildfire (rare): burns last 2 extra turns and burned foes can't heal (drain, berries, potions).

**Frost (4)**
- Permafrost: freeze lasts 1 extra turn; frozen foes take +25%.
- Hailguard: Frost Hexlings take 20% less from slower foes.
- Brittle: Frost moves have a 30% chance to lower Defense one step.
- Glacier (rare): Frost Hexlings start each battle at +2 Defense.

**Toxic (4)**
- Virulence: poison deals double damage.
- Contagion: Toxic moves +25% poison chance, and poison reaches Metal.
- Venom Sac: Toxic Hexlings deal +25% to poisoned foes.
- Plague Bearer (rare): the foe's lead starts each battle poisoned.

**Spirit (4)**
- Haunting: when an ally faints, its spirit hits the foe for 20% of the foe's max HP.
- Ethereal: Spirit Hexlings dodge the first attack each battle.
- Soul Link: a Spirit Hexling's knockout heals the team 10%.
- Possession (rare): Spirit moves have a 25% chance to confuse.

**Shadow (3)**
- Ambush: your first move each battle deals +50%.
- Nightfall: Shadow Hexlings gain +1 Speed when a foe faints; your next Hexling enters at +1 Speed when one of yours faints.
- Dread (rare): foes facing a Shadow Hexling deal 15% less.

**Nature (3)**
- Overgrowth: Nature Hexlings heal 1/16 HP each turn.
- Rootbound: Nature Hexlings' stats can't be lowered.
- Bloom (rare): resting heals the whole team (bench too) to full.

**Storm (3)**
- Static Field: foes that hit a Storm Hexling have a 20% chance to be paralyzed.
- Tailwind: Storm Hexlings +20% Speed.
- Chain Lightning (rare): Storm moves have a 20% chance to strike twice.

**Water (3)**
- Undertow: Water moves lower Speed one step.
- Tidecall: after a win, Water Hexlings restore uses of their strongest move.
- Riptide (rare): Water moves +30% against foes faster than the user.

**Earth (2)**
- Bedrock: the first hit each Hexling takes per battle is halved.
- Quake (rare): Earth moves ignore the foe's Defense raises and Bulwark.

**Dragon (2)**
- Hoard: Dragon moves +5% per 100 coins held (up to +25%).
- Dragon's Pride (rare): +15% to all stats while your lead is a Dragon.

**Light (2)**
- Radiance: after a win, cure the team's status and heal 10%.
- Halo (rare): Light Hexlings can't be crit and their stats can't be lowered.

**Metal (2)**
- Forged: Metal Hexlings gain +1 Defense each turn (up to +3).
- Ironclad (rare): Metal Hexlings reflect 15% of damage taken.

Boons only ever depend on your own team or the Hexling you're facing: many fights are a single
wild Hexling, so nothing relies on the foe's next Hexling or on foes switching.

### General boons (14)

- Team shape: **Pack Tactics** (+8% damage per teammate sharing an element with the attacker),
  **Glass Menagerie** (team capped at 3, everyone +40% Attack), **Rainbow Squad** (4 different
  elements on the team: +20% all stats), **Lone Champion** (lead +10% all stats per fainted or
  empty slot), **Bench Press** (bench gets full XP and switches in at +1 Attack).
- Status and tempo: **Lingering** (statuses you inflict last 2 extra turns), **Opportunist**
  (+30% against statused foes), **Momentum** (each knockout without switching: +15% damage for
  the battle), **First Strike** (you always move first on turn 1).
- Economy and run: **Scavenger** (each catch heals the team 15%), **Prospector** (+40% battle
  coins), **Haggler's Mark** (one free reroll per shop), **Field Medic** (potions heal 75%),
  **Pathfinder** (see hex types 2 rows further ahead; +1 treasure hex per floor).

### Mythic boons (5, about 1 in 10 offers)

- **Echo:** your first move each battle hits twice.
- **Overflow:** each knockout refunds that move's use and gives the attacker +1 Attack for the battle.
- **Second Wind:** once per floor, a Hexling that would faint comes back at 30%.
- **Elemental Fusion:** pick two elements; moves of either count as both for effectiveness.
- **Pact:** −25% max HP for everyone; every later offer is rare or mythic.

### Curses (8, always a drawback paired with a reward)

- **Blood Price:** rest hexes no longer heal. Take 2 boons now.
- **Fragile:** −20% max HP. Your next offer is all rare/mythic.
- **Bankrupt:** lose all your coins. Take 1 mythic now.
- **Haunted Floor:** every battle this floor is a trainer. +50% XP and coins this floor.
- **Short Fuse:** potions are disabled for the run. Everyone +25% Attack.
- **Exiled:** no catching this floor. +2 Limit Breakers in the next shop, or 2 Prestige stars.
- **Marked:** guardians +5 levels. Every guardian offer has 4 choices.
- **Fog of War:** you only see hex types 1 row ahead for the rest of the run. Take 2 rare boons now.

### Build

1. **Data** (`src/data/boons.ts`): about 63 boons, each with an id, name, rarity, element (if any),
   description, icon and effect data (numbers and triggers, not scattered code); curses also carry
   their drawback and reward.
2. **What's saved:** the run keeps its boons, the offer on screen (a reload can't reroll it) and
   curse flags (rests don't heal, potions off, fog, …). The profile keeps every boon ever seen (for
   the Hexlidex tab) and pick counts (for Records). Older saves start with none.
3. **Offers:** generated per the rules above from the run's seed.
4. **Effects:** one function turns the run's boons into a summary of their effects. The battle
   engine (`battle.ts`) reads it where it already applies synergy and held items (damage,
   accuracy/immunity, status chance and duration, turn start and end, knockouts, fainting, battle
   start, win). The map and run code cover coins, rerolls, potion strength, rests, how far ahead
   hexes show, extra treasure, and the guardian level bump. Curses apply when chosen and set flags.
5. **Online:** your boons travel with your team when a climb ends; the final duel and bracket
   matches apply each side's boons. Both phones must agree on every result, so boon effects draw
   only on the battle's shared seed. An online version bump stops mismatched versions from meeting.
6. **Screens:** the boon pick (three cards with rarity colour, element tag and effect; curses with
   their drawback and reward in red and green); boon icons in the map's top bar (tap for the list); the
   battle menu's boon list, and the stats panel noting boons that affect that Hexling; the end
   screen's boons this run; a Hexlidex **Boons** tab (found, ??? for the rest, mythics found);
   How to play, and a tip at the first offer.
7. **Balance:** the balance bot picks boons (its team's elements first; curses only for a mythic
   or 2 boons). Rerun the report. Targets: Fire/Frost/Toxic/Spirit within about 2 points of
   Earth/Dragon/Light; no boon with an outsized pick or win rate; curses worth taking. Tune the
   guardian bump if runs get too easy.
8. **Tests:** every boon is valid and the per-element counts hold; offers are seeded and follow
   the slot rules; each effect kind does what its description says in a hand-made battle; each
   curse applies its drawback and reward; saves survive reloads without rerolling an open offer;
   online, both sides compute identical results with boons in play.

**Order:** (1) data, saving, offers and the pick screen with a few simple boons, playable from end
to end; (2) battle hooks and the full list; (3) curses, the Boon hex and hunt offers; (4) online;
(5) the remaining screens; (6) balance bot, report and tuning.

## 2. Traits

Every species line gets its own personality on top of element, stats and signature move.

### Rules

- **Three possible traits per line:** two common (about 42.5% each) and one **rare** (15%, marked ◆).
- **Rolled when the Hexling is created** (wild, caught, starter, recruit, egg) from the run's seed,
  so seeded runs and online races stay identical for everyone. It's kept through evolution.
- **Final form upgrades the trait** (e.g. 15% → 25%).
- **Everyone has one:** wild Hexlings, trainers and guardians too (guardians get the upgraded
  version). A foe's trait shows on its battle stats panel.
- **Visible before you commit:** Hexlidex card (once seen), starter pick (the rolled trait),
  catch screen, team screen.
- **Collecting:** the Hexlidex shows which of a line's traits you've found ("2/3 traits"). In the
  **Arena** you can give a Hexling any trait you've found for its line.
- **Online and Arena:** traits apply.
- **Hand-picked:** each line's three fit its name, art and signature move. They're roughly equal in
  strength (the roll isn't a lottery; the rare one is a fun twist, not a must-have). Weaker elements
  and lower-stat lines get one strong option in their set.
- Like boons, no trait relies on the foe's next Hexling or on foes switching.

### Pool (about 70 traits)

**Hitters:** Heavy Hitter (+20% with 70+ power moves), Technician (+30% with ≤45 power), Adaptable
(same-element moves +15%), Berserk (+25% Attack below half HP), Predator (+20% vs foes below half
HP), Eager (first move each battle +30%), Overload (+10% per turn in, up to +30%), Piercing (resisted
hits ×0.75 instead of ×0.5).

**Tanks:** Thick Hide (−15% from super-effective hits), Steadfast (survives one knockout per battle
from above 75% HP), Regenerator (heals 25% when switched out), Resilient (statuses last half as long),
Scaled (−20% from faster foes), Rough Skin (attackers lose 1/10 of their max HP), Unshakable (stats
can't be lowered).

**Absorbers:** one per element (Lightning Rod, Sponge, Ember Eater, …): hits of that element heal it
25% instead. Lets a line soak one of its three weaknesses; weaker elements get these first.

**Tempo:** Quick Start (enters at +2 Speed for 2 turns), Momentum Runner (+1 Speed per knockout it
lands), Slipstream (moves first below 1/3 HP), Late Bloomer (+1 Attack and Defense every 2 turns),
Patient (+30% when moving second), Hit and Run (+1 Speed after a super-effective hit).

**Status:** Element Touch (15% chance its hits inflict its element's status), Purifier (30% chance a
turn to shake off its status), Guts (+25% while statused), Clear Mind (immune to sleep and
confusion), Mirror (a foe that statuses it gets the same status), Leech (heals 10% of damage dealt).

**Team:** Cheerleader (while benched, your active Hexling +5% damage), Healer's Touch (after a win
the team heals 5%), Avenger (+30% the turn after an ally faints), Mentor's Aura (bench +25% XP),
Kindred (counts twice for synergy), Lucky (+15% coins from its battles).

**Run perks:** Catcher (catches while it leads join +2 levels), Gilded Charm (better Gilded odds),
Forager (heals 10% after each battle), Scout (reveals the next hex's contents), Treasure Nose
(chests offer a 3rd choice), Steady (can't miss at full HP).

**Element-flavoured (about 15):** Magma Skin (attackers may be burned), Frostbite Aura (attackers may
be slowed), Static Coat (attackers may be paralyzed), Toxic Spines (attackers may be poisoned),
Tidal Grace (heals when using a Water move), Iron Will (+Defense below half HP), Night Stalker
(+Speed at night), Sunseeker (+Attack in sun), and others in the same spirit.

**Rare-only:** Second Skin (survives one knockout per battle at any HP), Wildcard (its signature
move takes the target's weakest element), Gilded Charm, and a few more.

**Legendaries:** each gets a unique trait (e.g. Solmane's Daybreak: sets sun on entering).

Examples: Emberkit line, Eager / Hit and Run / ◆ Kindred. Magmite line, Magma Skin / Late Bloomer / ◆
Ember Eater. Snowmote line, Element Touch (freeze) / Quick Start / ◆ Slipstream. Sproutle line,
Regenerator / Unshakable / ◆ Healer's Touch.

### Build

1. **Review table first:** draft all 120 lines' three traits, grouped by element with names, stats and
   signature, for review and sign-off before any code.
2. **Data:** `src/data/traits.ts` (each trait: name, description, base and final-form numbers,
   effect data, tags) and each species' three trait ids (two common, one rare).
3. **Saved on the Hexling:** a `trait` field, rolled at creation from the run's seed and the
   Hexling's id. Older saves get one rolled from the uid on load. Online, it travels with the Hexling.
4. **Effects:** traits plug into the same effect summary as boons (battle hooks, map and run hooks),
   so they share one code path. The final form uses the stronger numbers.
5. **Screens:** a trait line on the battle stats panel, team screen, catch screen, starter pick and
   Hexlidex card (with "found 2/3" and ◆ for rare); Arena editor lets you pick a found trait.
6. **Balance:** the bot runs with traits (passive, no strategy needed). Report by trait: win rate
   and how often it's on the winning team. Rebalance outliers; check the weaker elements move up.
7. **Tests:** every line has 2 common + 1 rare valid trait; rolls are seeded and keep the 42.5/42.5/15
   split; evolution keeps the trait and upgrades the numbers; each effect kind works in a hand-made
   battle; old saves get a trait.

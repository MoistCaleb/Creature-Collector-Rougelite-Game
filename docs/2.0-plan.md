# Hexbound 2.0 plan

2.0 is one large update. Every section below is built on the working branch behind a single draft
PR, and nothing reaches main (or the live game) until all of 2.0 is ready. Fixes to the live game
still ship on their own from a short-lived branch and are merged into the 2.0 work.

Why 2.0: one run and you've "seen it all". Runs need their own character: builds that snowball
from choices made during the run.

Sections:

1. **Boons** (planned below)
2. **Traits** (planned below)
3. **Rare surprises** (planned below)
4. **Every run moves you forward** (planned below)
5. **Launch and polish** (planned below)
6. **Art remaster and 66 new lines** (planned below)

**Overall build order:** save-safety patch (ships now) → art style test → generator upgrade and
remaster of the 120 lines → design the 66 new lines (review table) → traits review table for all
186 → traits → boons (with callouts and sound) → surprises → progression → quick runs, records,
leaderboards, titles, tour and the final balance pass.

---

**Also in 2.0 (asked for along the way):** speed attacks (Blitz / Crush) scale more gently: power
60 when evenly matched, about 76 at 1.5× and at most 90 at twice as fast (was 70 / 110 / 130), down
to 40 at half speed. Done (`speedPower` in `src/data/moves.ts`).

## 1. Boons

Rule-bending perks for one run that stack into a build. 3–5 per run.

### Offers

- **When:** after each floor's guardian (3 per run, before the next floor); a **Boon hex** on about
  1 floor in 2 (at most one per floor, placed by a hash of the floor's layout like Revival, so the
  rest of the map is unchanged); in the Gilded Hunt, an offer every 2 hunt floors.
- **Choice of 3:** slot 1 matches an element on your team (weighted toward the weaker elements);
  slot 2 is a general boon (about 10% mythic); slot 3 is a curse about 25% of the time, else another
  general boon. No duplicates, nothing you already hold.
- **Seeded** like shop stock (`placeRng`): online races and daily/weekly runs offer everyone the
  same boons at the same points.
- **Online:** boons apply in stage battles and battle royales, including the final duel and the
  bracket (each player brings the boons they picked on their climb).
- **Guardians** get +1 level per boon held so stacked runs stay honest.

### Element boons (36)

Bot win rates by starter element (BALANCE_REPORT, 2026-11-19): Earth 20%, Dragon 18%, Light 18%,
Metal 17%, Water/Storm/Nature/Shadow 16%, Spirit/Toxic 15%, Fire/Frost 14%. The weaker elements get
more boons, and slightly stronger ones.

**Fire (4)**
- Kindling: Fire moves +20% burn chance; burned foes take +20% from Fire.
- Heat Wave: after a win, Fire Hexlings heal 15% (30% in sun).
- Ember Heart: Fire Hexlings can't be frozen and take 25% less from Water.
- Wildfire (rare): burns last 2 extra turns and burned foes can't heal (drain, berries, potions).

**Frost (4)**
- Permafrost: freeze lasts 1 extra turn; frozen foes take +25%.
- Hailguard: Frost Hexlings take 20% less from slower foes.
- Brittle: Frost moves have a 30% chance to lower Defense one step.
- Glacier (rare): Frost Hexlings start each battle at +2 Defense.

**Toxic (4)**
- Virulence: poison deals double damage.
- Contagion: Toxic moves +25% poison chance, and poison reaches Metal.
- Venom Sac: Toxic Hexlings deal +25% to poisoned foes.
- Plague Bearer (rare): the foe's lead starts each battle poisoned.

**Spirit (4)**
- Haunting: when an ally faints, its spirit hits the foe for 20% of the foe's max HP.
- Ethereal: Spirit Hexlings dodge the first attack each battle.
- Soul Link: a Spirit Hexling's knockout heals the team 10%.
- Possession (rare): Spirit moves have a 25% chance to confuse.

**Shadow (3)**
- Ambush: your first move each battle deals +50%.
- Nightfall: Shadow Hexlings gain +1 Speed when a foe faints; your next Hexling enters at +1 Speed when one of yours faints.
- Dread (rare): foes facing a Shadow Hexling deal 15% less.

**Nature (3)**
- Overgrowth: Nature Hexlings heal 1/16 HP each turn.
- Rootbound: Nature Hexlings' stats can't be lowered.
- Bloom (rare): resting heals the whole team (bench too) to full.

**Storm (3)**
- Static Field: foes that hit a Storm Hexling have a 20% chance to be paralyzed.
- Tailwind: Storm Hexlings +20% Speed.
- Chain Lightning (rare): Storm moves have a 20% chance to strike twice.

**Water (3)**
- Undertow: Water moves lower Speed one step.
- Tidecall: after a win, Water Hexlings restore uses of their strongest move.
- Riptide (rare): Water moves +30% against foes faster than the user.

**Earth (2)**
- Bedrock: the first hit each Hexling takes per battle is halved.
- Quake (rare): Earth moves ignore the foe's Defense raises and Bulwark.

**Dragon (2)**
- Hoard: Dragon moves +5% per 100 coins held (up to +25%).
- Dragon's Pride (rare): +15% to all stats while your lead is a Dragon.

**Light (2)**
- Radiance: after a win, cure the team's status and heal 10%.
- Halo (rare): Light Hexlings can't be crit and their stats can't be lowered.

**Metal (2)**
- Forged: Metal Hexlings gain +1 Defense each turn (up to +3).
- Ironclad (rare): Metal Hexlings reflect 15% of damage taken.

Boons only ever depend on your own team or the Hexling you're facing: many fights are a single
wild Hexling, so nothing relies on the foe's next Hexling or on foes switching.

### General boons (14)

- Team shape: **Pack Tactics** (+8% damage per teammate sharing an element with the attacker),
  **Glass Menagerie** (team capped at 3, everyone +40% Attack), **Rainbow Squad** (4 different
  elements on the team: +20% all stats), **Lone Champion** (lead +10% all stats per fainted or
  empty slot), **Bench Press** (bench gets full XP and switches in at +1 Attack).
- Status and tempo: **Lingering** (statuses you inflict last 2 extra turns), **Opportunist**
  (+30% against statused foes), **Momentum** (each knockout without switching: +15% damage for
  the battle), **First Strike** (you always move first on turn 1).
- Economy and run: **Scavenger** (each catch heals the team 15%), **Prospector** (+40% battle
  coins), **Haggler's Mark** (one free reroll per shop), **Field Medic** (potions heal 75%),
  **Pathfinder** (see hex types 2 rows further ahead; +1 treasure hex per floor).

### Mythic boons (5, about 1 in 10 offers)

- **Echo:** your first move each battle hits twice.
- **Overflow:** each knockout refunds that move's use and gives the attacker +1 Attack for the battle.
- **Second Wind:** once per floor, a Hexling that would faint comes back at 30%.
- **Elemental Fusion:** pick two elements; moves of either count as both for effectiveness.
- **Pact:** −25% max HP for everyone; every later offer is rare or mythic.

### Curses (8, always a drawback paired with a reward)

- **Blood Price:** rest hexes no longer heal. Take 2 boons now.
- **Fragile:** −20% max HP. Your next offer is all rare/mythic.
- **Bankrupt:** lose all your coins. Take 1 mythic now.
- **Haunted Floor:** every battle this floor is a trainer. +50% XP and coins this floor.
- **Short Fuse:** potions are disabled for the run. Everyone +25% Attack.
- **Exiled:** no catching this floor. +2 Limit Breakers in the next shop, or 2 Prestige stars.
- **Marked:** guardians +5 levels. Every guardian offer has 4 choices.
- **Fog of War:** you only see hex types 1 row ahead for the rest of the run. Take 2 rare boons now.

### Build

1. **Data** (`src/data/boons.ts`): about 63 boons, each with an id, name, rarity, element (if any),
   description, icon and effect data (numbers and triggers, not scattered code); curses also carry
   their drawback and reward.
2. **What's saved:** the run keeps its boons, the offer on screen (a reload can't reroll it) and
   curse flags (rests don't heal, potions off, fog, …). The profile keeps every boon ever seen (for
   the Hexlidex tab) and pick counts (for Records). Older saves start with none.
3. **Offers:** generated per the rules above from the run's seed.
4. **Effects:** one function turns the run's boons into a summary of their effects. The battle
   engine (`battle.ts`) reads it where it already applies synergy and held items (damage,
   accuracy/immunity, status chance and duration, turn start and end, knockouts, fainting, battle
   start, win). The map and run code cover coins, rerolls, potion strength, rests, how far ahead
   hexes show, extra treasure, and the guardian level bump. Curses apply when chosen and set flags.
5. **Online:** your boons travel with your team when a climb ends; the final duel and bracket
   matches apply each side's boons. Both phones must agree on every result, so boon effects draw
   only on the battle's shared seed. An online version bump stops mismatched versions from meeting.
6. **Screens:** the boon pick (three cards with rarity colour, element tag and effect; curses with
   their drawback and reward in red and green); boon icons in the map's top bar (tap for the list); the
   battle menu's boon list, and the stats panel noting boons that affect that Hexling; the end
   screen's boons this run; a Hexlidex **Boons** tab (found, ??? for the rest, mythics found);
   How to play, and a tip at the first offer.
7. **Balance:** the balance bot picks boons (its team's elements first; curses only for a mythic
   or 2 boons). Rerun the report. Targets: Fire/Frost/Toxic/Spirit within about 2 points of
   Earth/Dragon/Light; no boon with an outsized pick or win rate; curses worth taking. Tune the
   guardian bump if runs get too easy.
8. **Tests:** every boon is valid and the per-element counts hold; offers are seeded and follow
   the slot rules; each effect kind does what its description says in a hand-made battle; each
   curse applies its drawback and reward; saves survive reloads without rerolling an open offer;
   online, both sides compute identical results with boons in play.

**Order:** (1) data, saving, offers and the pick screen with a few simple boons, playable from end
to end; (2) battle hooks and the full list; (3) curses, the Boon hex and hunt offers; (4) online;
(5) the remaining screens; (6) balance bot, report and tuning.

> **Status (built):** all 63 boons are in (`src/data/boons.ts`, `src/logic/boons.ts`), with the pick
> screen, callouts and sounds, the Boon hex, hunt offers, curses, online duels/brackets, the map chip,
> menu list, Hexlidex Boons tab and end screen. Renamed so they don't clash with traits: Ethereal →
> **Phantom Step**, Momentum → **Rampage**, Quake → **Fault Line**. Changed to fit the game: Halo
> (no crits in the game) is "take 15% less, stats can't be lowered"; Elemental Fusion needs no
> picking ("hits with whichever of its element or the user's works best"); Pathfinder (the map shows
> every hex already) shows who waits on the battle hexes next to you, plus a treasure hex; Exiled's
> reward is 2 Prestige stars. Guardians keep +1 level per boon (decided). Bot report:
> `docs/BOONS_REPORT.md` (9% → 10% for the bot, which rarely lives to hold more than one).

## 2. Traits

Every species line gets its own personality on top of element, stats and signature move.

### Rules

- **Three possible traits per line:** two common (about 42.5% each) and one **rare** (15%, marked ◆).
- **Rolled when the Hexling is created** (wild, caught, starter, recruit, egg) from the run's seed,
  so seeded runs and online races stay identical for everyone. It's kept through evolution.
- **Final form upgrades the trait** (e.g. 15% → 25%).
- **Everyone has one:** wild Hexlings, trainers and guardians too (guardians get the upgraded
  version). A foe's trait shows on its battle stats panel.
- **Visible before you commit:** Hexlidex card (once seen), starter pick (the rolled trait),
  catch screen, team screen.
- **Collecting:** the Hexlidex shows which of a line's traits you've found ("2/3 traits"). In the
  **Arena** you can give a Hexling any trait you've found for its line.
- **Online and Arena:** traits apply.
- **Hand-picked:** each line's three fit its name, art and signature move. They're roughly equal in
  strength (the roll isn't a lottery; the rare one is a fun twist, not a must-have). Weaker elements
  and lower-stat lines get one strong option in their set.
- Like boons, no trait relies on the foe's next Hexling or on foes switching.

### Pool (about 70 traits)

**Hitters:** Heavy Hitter (+20% with 70+ power moves), Technician (+30% with ≤45 power), Adaptable
(same-element moves +15%), Berserk (+25% Attack below half HP), Predator (+20% vs foes below half
HP), Eager (first move each battle +30%), Overload (+10% per turn in, up to +30%), Piercing (resisted
hits ×0.75 instead of ×0.5).

**Tanks:** Thick Hide (−15% from super-effective hits), Steadfast (survives one knockout per battle
from above 75% HP), Regenerator (heals 25% when switched out), Resilient (statuses last half as long),
Scaled (−20% from faster foes), Rough Skin (attackers lose 1/10 of their max HP), Unshakable (stats
can't be lowered).

**Absorbers:** one per element (Lightning Rod, Sponge, Ember Eater, …): hits of that element heal it
25% instead. Lets a line soak one of its three weaknesses; weaker elements get these first.

**Tempo:** Quick Start (enters at +2 Speed for 2 turns), Momentum Runner (+1 Speed per knockout it
lands), Slipstream (moves first below 1/3 HP), Late Bloomer (+1 Attack and Defense every 2 turns),
Patient (+30% when moving second), Hit and Run (+1 Speed after a super-effective hit).

**Status:** Element Touch (15% chance its hits inflict its element's status), Purifier (30% chance a
turn to shake off its status), Guts (+25% while statused), Mirror (a foe that statuses it gets the same status), Leech (heals 10% of damage dealt).

**Team:** Cheerleader (while benched, your active Hexling +5% damage), Healer's Touch (after a win
the team heals 5%), Avenger (+30% the turn after an ally faints), Mentor's Aura (bench +25% XP),
Kindred (counts twice for synergy), Lucky (+15% coins from its battles).

**Run perks:** Catcher (catches while it leads join +2 levels), Gilded Charm (better Gilded odds),
Forager (heals 10% after each battle), Scout (reveals the next hex's contents), Treasure Nose
(chests offer a 3rd choice), Steady (can't miss at full HP).

**Element-flavoured (about 15):** Magma Skin (attackers may be burned), Frostbite Aura (attackers may
be slowed), Static Coat (attackers may be paralyzed), Toxic Spines (attackers may be poisoned),
Tidal Grace (heals when using a Water move), Iron Will (+Defense below half HP), Night Stalker
(night falls for the battle when it enters; +Speed at night), Sunseeker / Rain Rider / Sand Shroud
(bring 2 turns of their weather when they enter, 3 at final form, and get their bonus in it), and
others in the same spirit. Battles get a **night** state (set by Night Stalker and Umbrawyrm), never
the real clock, so online battles play the same for everyone.

**Rare-only:** Second Skin (survives one knockout per battle at any HP), Wildcard (its signature
move takes the target's weakest element), Gilded Charm, and a few more.

**Legendaries:** each gets a unique trait (e.g. Solmane's Daybreak: sets sun on entering).

Examples: Emberkit line, Eager / Hit and Run / ◆ Kindred. Magmite line, Magma Skin / Late Bloomer / ◆
Ember Eater. Snowmote line, Element Touch (freeze) / Quick Start / ◆ Slipstream. Sproutle line,
Regenerator / Unshakable / ◆ Healer's Touch.

### Build

1. **Review table first** (draft: `docs/2.0-traits.md`, all 186 lines + 6 legendaries): draft all lines' three traits, grouped by element with names, stats and
   signature, for review and sign-off before any code.
2. **Data:** `src/data/traits.ts` (each trait: name, description, base and final-form numbers,
   effect data, tags) and each species' three trait ids (two common, one rare).
3. **Saved on the Hexling:** a `trait` field, rolled at creation from the run's seed and the
   Hexling's id. Older saves get one rolled from the uid on load. Online, it travels with the Hexling.
4. **Effects:** traits plug into the same effect summary as boons (battle hooks, map and run hooks),
   so they share one code path. The final form uses the stronger numbers.
5. **Screens:** a trait line on the battle stats panel, team screen, catch screen, starter pick and
   Hexlidex card (with "found 2/3" and ◆ for rare); Arena editor lets you pick a found trait.
6. **Balance:** the bot runs with traits (passive, no strategy needed). Report by trait: win rate
   and how often it's on the winning team. Rebalance outliers; check the weaker elements move up.
7. **Tests:** every line has 2 common + 1 rare valid trait; rolls are seeded and keep the 42.5/42.5/15
   split; evolution keeps the trait and upgrades the numbers; each effect kind works in a hand-made
   battle; old saves get a trait.

## 3. Rare surprises

Things you might not see for ten runs, so a run can always surprise you.

### Rules

- **About 1 run in 2 has at least one surprise;** any specific one is 1 in 10 to 40 runs. Each floor
  gets a seeded roll.
- **Bad-luck protection:** each run without a surprise raises the odds, so it's never more than about
  3 runs without one (a counter on the profile).
- **Seeded:** daily/weekly runs and online races (stage battles and battle royales included) give
  everyone the same surprises. The two personal ones (Echo of yourself and a friend's ghost) can't be
  identical for everyone, so in seeded runs they're swapped for another rare encounter.
- **Collection:** a Surprises page in the Hexlidex (found, or ??? with a vague hint), and a title for
  finding them all.
- **Announced:** a banner for special floors, a sparkle on hidden hexes, a different entrance for
  rare guardians.

### What can happen

**Rare floors (1 in 15–25 runs each):**
- **Gilded Grove:** gold-tinted floor, Gilded odds ×5, the shop sells Gilded Lures.
- **Eclipse:** permanent night, only Shadow/Spirit Hexlings, the guardian is the Eclipse version.
- **Storm Front:** weather in every battle (rotating through the game's weathers), weather effects doubled.
- **Mirror Floor:** wild Hexlings are copies of your team's species (different traits).
- **Ancient Ruins:** a legendary is guaranteed to roam, ruins hexes hold relics, the guardian is a stone titan.
- **Shattered Floor:** two lands at once; wild Hexlings mix both, and the guardian brings both.

**Elder guardians (1 in 8 per floor):** a rare version of each of the 12 guardians: a different team
(rare traits mixed in), a unique rule, their own dialogue, and a better reward (a guaranteed mythic
offer or a guardian-themed camp decoration). Beating all 12 Elders earns a title.

**Hidden hexes (about 1 floor in 6, at most one per floor):**
- **Hidden Shop:** boons (pricey), **Trait Scroll** (reroll a trait), **Gilded Lure**, and Limit
  Breakers before hunt floor 6.
- **Cursed Altar:** sacrifice a Hexling or half your coins for a mythic boon.
- **Wishing Well:** pay coins for a random result, from nothing to a mythic boon or a Gilded recruit.
- **Hatchery:** an egg that hatches after 5 battles into a line you haven't caught, always with its
  rare ◆ trait.
- **Dojo:** three trainer battles in a row, no healing between; win a boon and a Limit Breaker.
- **Fossil Site:** an **ancient form**, a rare alternate-colour version of a line, with its own
  Hexlidex entry like Gilded.

**Rare encounters (a battle hex becomes one):**
- **Echo of yourself:** a trainer fielding your last winning team (from your run history), at this
  floor's level.
- **A friend's ghost:** a trainer fielding a friend's last winning team, with their name and look.
  Beat it for a souvenir from them. Works while they're offline.
- **Gilded trainer:** every Hexling on their team is Gilded.
- **Twin wilds:** two of the same species, both with the rare ◆ trait.
- **Travelling caravan:** a 7-item shop with one free item.

**Omens (1 in 15 runs, announced at the start):**
- **Blood Moon:** every enemy has its rare trait; every boon offer has 4 choices.
- **Golden Age:** coins ×2, prices ×1.5.
- **Wild Surge:** wild Hexlings 3 levels higher, but a catch below 25% HP never fails.
- **Starfall:** every floor has a Boon hex.

They feed the other sections: boons from the hidden shop, altar, well, Elders and omens; traits from
Trait Scrolls, twin wilds, the Hatchery and Blood Moon.

### Build

1. **Data** (`src/data/surprises.ts`): every surprise with its kind (floor, guardian, hex, encounter,
   omen), odds, hint text and reward, and Elder guardian definitions (team, rule, lines, reward).
2. **Rolls:** per run (omen) and per floor (floor variant, Elder, hidden hex, encounter), seeded like
   shops, plus the bad-luck counter on the profile. Hidden hexes are placed by a hash of the floor's
   layout like Revival, so the rest of the map is unchanged.
3. **Saved:** the run's omen, each floor's surprises, an egg's battles left, items like Trait
   Scrolls and Lures; the profile keeps surprises found and the bad-luck counter.
4. **Floors and encounters:** floor variants change the encounter pools, weather, tint and guardian;
   Elders replace the guardian's team, rule and lines; encounters reuse trainer battles with their own
   teams.
5. **New hex screens:** hidden shop (reuses the shop), altar, well, Hatchery, Dojo (chained trainer
   battles), fossil; ancient forms (an alternate palette and Hexlidex entries, like Gilded).
6. **Friend ghosts (server):** a small SQL addition (the player runs it) saves each player's last
   winning team on their profile, and friends can read it (same as buddy and look today). Uploaded
   when you win a run.
7. **Screens:** banners and entrances, hidden-hex sparkle, the Hexlidex Surprises page, the omen at
   the starter pick, How to play.
8. **Balance:** the bot meets surprises too; check that win rates on surprise floors stay in line and
   that the rewards aren't must-haves.
9. **Tests:** rolls are seeded and the never-more-than-3-runs guarantee holds; each surprise applies
   its rules and reward; hidden hexes don't change the rest of the map; saves survive reloads; in
   seeded runs the personal encounters are swapped; ghosts load from a faked server response.

> **Status (part built):** `src/data/surprises.ts`, `src/logic/surprises.ts`. Built: seeded omens
> (Blood Moon, Golden Age, Wild Surge, Starfall), rare floors (Gilded Grove, Eclipse, Storm Front,
> Mirror, Shattered), Elder guardians (+2 levels, an extra rule, rare traits, a mythic offer),
> hidden hexes (Wishing Well, Cursed Altar, Hidden Shop), rare encounters (Gilded trainer, Twin wilds),
> the 3-run drought guarantee (unseeded runs), banners, sparkles, a Hexlidex surprises list and tests.
> Odds per floor: rare floor 1/30, Elder 1/20, hidden hex 1/12, encounter 1/20; omen 1/15 a run.
> **Still to build:** Ancient Ruins, Hatchery, Dojo, Fossil Site / ancient forms, Echo of yourself,
> friend's ghost (needs SQL), Travelling caravan, Elder dialogue and camp decorations, the "found them
> all" and "all 12 Elders" titles, the omen at the starter pick, How to play, and a bot pass.

## 4. Every run moves you forward

Every run, even a quick loss, earns something, and there's always a next goal close enough to want
one more run.

### Embers from every run

At the end of any run, win or lose: 5 per floor cleared, +3 per guardian beaten, +15 for winning,
+10% per Heat level, +2 per Gilded caught, +1 per new Hexlidex entry, +3 per Gilded Hunt floor.
About 8–15 for a loss and 35–50 for a win (daily challenges give about 21 a day; decorations cost
6–40).

**End screen:** an ember tally counting up line by line, a progress bar to the next upgrade, and a
**Run again** button straight to the starter pick (or camp prep) on the same map.

### The Workshop (camp upgrades)

A new camp stall. Four branches, cheap and modest early, steeply pricier later, each ending in a
**capstone**. About **5,000 embers** in all, roughly 120–150 runs.

- **Supplies:** +1 starting potion (I–II), +40 starting coins (I–III), a free shop reroll per floor,
  a bigger potion pouch. **Capstone:** start every run with a Limit Breaker and a full pouch.
- **Training:** starter +1 level (I–III), more bench XP, Revival brings Hexlings back at 70%, an
  extra bench slot. **Capstone (Partner):** at the starter pick you take two of the three
  starters; the second joins your team at the same level. (Not "always the rare trait": that would
  make bond heart 4's trait choice pointless.)
- **Fortune:** better boon rarity, a 4th choice on each run's first boon offer, better hidden-hex
  odds, better Gilded odds. **Capstone:** start every run with a boon (choose from 3).
- **Lore (variety, not power):** each tier adds boons to the offer pool, new events, rare floors,
  hidden hex types and Elder rewards. **Capstone:** the last mythics and the rarest surprises, plus
  one extra boon offer per run.

**Lore gating:** 2.0 starts with about 35 boons and the more common surprises; Lore unlocks the rest.

### Gilded Hunt milestones

One-time rewards for each new deepest-hunt floor: floor 3, 25 embers; floor 5, a Gilded camp fire
(and the existing Deep Diver title); floor 7, a **Gilded starter option** (one of the three starters
can be offered Gilded); floor 10, a hunt decoration and 100 embers; floors 12, 15, … escalating
embers and titles.

### Bond, deepened (line mastery)

Camp bond becomes the long-term per-line goal, with **5 hearts** that come slower.

- **Points:** caring at camp +1 a day; finishing a run with it on your team +2; winning +4; run MVP
  +3; each guardian it helps beat +1.
- **Hearts at about 10, 30, 60, 100, 150 points** (a favourite line takes about 25–35 runs to max).
- **Rewards:** heart 1, its camp form evolves and a heart shows on its Hexlidex card; heart 2, its
  final camp form; heart 3, it starts runs with a bond heart; heart 4, as your starter you **choose its
  trait** from the ones you've found; heart 5, it starts 2 levels higher as a starter, gets an
  **alternate colour**, and visits your camp.
- **Existing hearts are kept:** current bond points are converted to the new scale.

### Fairness

Daily/weekly runs and online races ignore Workshop upgrades and bond starter bonuses, and always use
the full boon and surprise pool. They still earn embers and bond.

### Build

1. **Embers:** a payout function from the run log; end-screen tally, progress bar and Run again.
2. **Workshop:** data for branches, tiers, costs and effects; a camp stall screen; effects applied when
   a run starts and through the shared effect summary (Fortune and Lore change boon and surprise
   offers; Lore filters the pools).
3. **Hunt milestones:** checked when a new deepest floor is reached; one-time rewards on the profile.
4. **Bond:** new point sources from run results, 5 hearts with the new thresholds, the reward
   hooks (trait choice at starter pick, starter level, alternate colour palette, camp visitor), and
   converting existing points.
5. **Screens:** the Workshop, bond hearts on Hexlidex cards and at camp, the trait choice at starter
   pick, How to play.
6. **Balance:** the bot gets a "veteran" mode with all upgrades, to make sure maxed players still
   lose sometimes, and a "new player" mode that confirms early runs feel the same as today.
7. **Tests:** payouts by run type; every upgrade's effect; seeded runs ignore upgrades and use the
   full pools; bond thresholds and conversion keep everyone's hearts; milestones pay once.

> **Status (part built):** Embers from every run (`runEmbers` / `payRunEmbers` in `src/logic/camp.ts`)
> and Run again on the end screen. **The Workshop** (`src/data/workshop.ts`, `src/logic/workshop.ts`,
> `tests/workshop.test.ts`): four branches of 1,250 embers (5,000 in all), bought in order, each with

## 5. Launch and polish

No separate preview build: 2.0 ships when it's done, and bugs are fixed in patch updates after.

### Showing what triggered

- **Callouts:** a short tag over the Hexling when a boon or trait fires ("Kindling!", "Thick Hide"),
  in the boon's or trait's colour, batched so a busy turn doesn't spam.
- **Battle log "why":** each line says what caused it, e.g. "Infernox's Ember: +20% (Kindling),
  burned (Kindling)" or "Glacimaw took 15% less (Thick Hide)".
- Callouts follow the battle speed and Reduce motion settings.

### Welcoming existing players

- **A short 2.0 tour** the first time 2.0 opens, then tips at the right moment: the first boon
  offer, traits on the starter pick, the Workshop and embers at camp, a surprise the first time one
  appears.
- **Welcome-back embers:** a one-time gift from past progress (e.g. 10 per past win, capped), so
  veterans don't start the Workshop from zero.

### Save safety (ships early, as a patch)

- Saves (local, cloud and save codes) carry the game version that wrote them.
- An older version won't overwrite a newer save: it stops syncing and asks the player to update. A
  save code from a newer version says to update first instead of loading.
- Shipped ahead of 2.0, so every device is protected by the time 2.0 arrives.

### New records and titles

- **Leaderboards:** Workshop completion, max-bond lines, surprises found, Elders beaten, mythic
  boons found (needs the leaderboard SQL rerun with the new boards).
- **Titles:** e.g. all 12 Elders, every surprise, a 5-heart line, the Workshop complete, a mythic of
  each kind.
- **Records page:** the same, plus boon pick counts and favourite trait.

### Sound

- New sounds: boon offer and pick (with a mythic fanfare and a darker curse sting), trait callouts

- New music: rare floors (Gilded Grove, Eclipse, Ancient Ruins, …), Elder guardians, the omens'

- Synthesized the same way as the current music.

### Quick runs

  all-time wins, unlocks or Heat progress.
- Embers scale down to match (about half), boons scale to 2–3 per run, and bond and the Hexlidex
  still count. Surprises can appear.

Part of the "creature generator" sameness is the art: every Hexling is built from a small set of
parts on a 40×40 canvas, so pieces repeat across the 360 forms.

### Generator upgrade

- **Larger canvas** (about 48–56 px) for more detail; display scales already adjust (`SPRITE_FIT`),
  so on-screen sizes stay the same.
- **About twice the parts** in each category (body plans, bodies, heads, arms, backs, tops, mouths,
  patterns, tails), plus **element features**: crystal growths (Frost), ember vents (Fire), circuit
  lines (Storm), drips (Toxic), wisps (Spirit), rivets and plates (Metal), and so on.
- **A signature part per line** that only that line has.
- Better shading (more tones, rim light), still the pixel-art look.
- Parts can echo traits where it fits (Magma Skin: cracked glowing plates; Thick Hide: armour).

### Remaster of the 120 lines

- Every line keeps its **silhouette and colours** so it stays recognisable; it gains detail, not a
  new identity.
- Lines are part plans, so the generator upgrade redraws them all; each plan is then hand-tuned.
- Gilded and ancient-form palettes, camp forms, battle and Hexlidex views all use the new art.

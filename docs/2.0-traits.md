# 2.0 traits: review table

Each line's three traits for sign-off before any code (section 2 of `2.0-plan.md`). Two are common (about 42.5% each) and one is **rare (◆, 15%)**. A Hexling rolls one when it's created and keeps it through evolution; its **final form** gets the stronger number.

**How the sets were picked**
- Each set fits the line's name, look and signature move (Pebble Barrage → Technician, Molet → Scout, Shellop → Thick Hide).
- The bot's weakest elements (**Fire and Frost 14%, Spirit and Toxic 15%** win rate) get at least one strong trait in every set: an absorber, Steadfast, Thick Hide, Ethereal, Second Skin or Wildcard.
- The strongest elements (**Earth 20%, Dragon and Light 18%**) get mostly utility and run traits.
- **Absorbers** always soak a hit the line is actually weak to (checked against the element chart). 12 of them, one per element.
- Nothing depends on the foe switching or on its next Hexling.

Lines marked ★ are the 66 new ones. Every trait in the pool is used at least once (65 traits, plus 6 legendary ones).

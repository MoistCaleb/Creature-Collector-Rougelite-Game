# Balance report

Generated by `scripts/balance.ts` on 2026-11-17: a bot played 9,900 runs (plus Gilded Hunts) in 53 s.

The bot plays decently but simply: best move by type and power, switches out of bad matchups, potions when low, rests when hurt, buys useful items. It ignores events and rarely catches. **Read the numbers as relative** (this starter vs that one, Heat 3 vs Heat 4). A thoughtful player wins more often than the bot.

Re-run: `npx vite-node scripts/balance.ts` (add `0.2` for a quick pass).
